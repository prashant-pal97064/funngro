Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Are the teenagers actually verified and skilled?

Yes! Every student goes through a profile verification and skill assessment before they can apply to projects. You only see profiles of teens who have proven their abilities in their chosen category.

How much does it cost to hire on Funngro?

Posting a project is completely free. You set your own budget when posting. Funngro charges a small platform fee on completed transactions to keep the service running. Teen rates are significantly lower than professional freelancers.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Payment is held in escrow until you approve the work. If the deliverable doesn't meet your requirements, you can request revisions. Our support team is available to help resolve any disputes fairly.

How quickly can I find someone for my project?

Most projects receive their first applications within a few hours of posting. Depending on the complexity and budget, you can typically hire someone within 24-48 hours.

Can I hire the same student for future projects?

Absolutely! If you find a student whose work you love, you can save their profile and invite them directly to your future projects. Many companies build long-term relationships with their Funngro talent.
