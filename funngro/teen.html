Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know before you start.

Who can join Funngro as a student?

Any student between 13 and 19 years old who has a skill they want to offer can join. You don't need to be a professional — you just need to be genuine, willing to work, and able to deliver what you promise.

How do I get paid?

Once a company approves your work, payment is released to your Funngro wallet. You can withdraw it to your bank account or UPI. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to verify the payout details once during setup.

Do I need prior work experience?

Nope! Many students on Funngro got their very first gig here. Build a solid profile by adding personal projects, school work, or anything you've made on your own. Companies are looking for talent and attitude — not just a resume.

Will this affect my studies?

You're fully in control of how much you take on. Apply only to projects that fit your schedule. Most students work 5–10 hours a week and find that real project experience actually helps them understand their school subjects better.

What if a company is rude or doesn't pay?

Funngro uses an escrow system — companies deposit payment before work begins, so you're always protected. If there's any issue with a client's behaviour, our support team will step in and help resolve it quickly.
